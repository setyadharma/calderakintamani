    Green Caldera Estate

    Green Caldera Estate is a villa located in Songan Kintamani Village Bangli Bali with a distance of approximately 85 km from Ngurah Rai Bali Airport. Green Caldera Estate is a villa with a building that is still new and with facilities that will certainly make you comfortable during your stay in Kintamani. Green Caldera Estate offers a different atmosphere from most villas in Kintamani because of the position of the villa adjacent to the green rice fields and close to Lake Batur so that when you open the window of the room then you will see the beautiful view of Caldera with cool natural air which will certainly make you comfortable and will arouse the spirit to start tourism activities while in Kintamani.

    Jeep

    Explore Mountain With Jeep Car

    Enjoy an Unforgettable Adventure!

    Starting from the Ebike Kintamani meeting point, our first staff or driver will give you a small briefing about JEEP TOURS before starting your jeep tour, this tour starts from the road and will pass black lava or frozen lava, black sand slide show and amazing scenery, our last sports local farmer community will pass through the forest at the foot of Mount Batur and end again at Ebike Kintamani. Black lava is frozen lava from mountain eruptions decades and hundreds of years ago, Black Lava is a real volcanic jeep adventure tour that takes you on a fun journey across this unique volcanic terrain. Your tour guide is a professional 4WD Jeep and driving specialist, which means you are in safe hands in this off-road adventure.

    Trunyan Boat Tour

    If you are a lover of nature and countryside tourism, the choice of Tour to Trunyan Village is certainly the right choice for you.Trunyan Village is a village in Kintamani District, Bangli, Bali, which has a unique funeral tradition. Residents who died were not buried or cremated, but only placed under the Taru Menyan tree. This tree will be able to remove the smell of corpses there. Depart from the Ebike Kintamani meeting point.
