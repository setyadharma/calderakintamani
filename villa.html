Description

Green Caldera Estate is a villa located in Songan Kintamani Village Bangli Bali with a distance of approximately 85 km from Ngurah Rai Bali Airport. Green Caldera Estate is a villa with a building that is still new and with facilities that will certainly make you comfortable during your stay in Kintamani. Green Caldera Estate offers a different atmosphere from most villas in Kintamani because of the position of the villa adjacent to the green rice fields and close to Lake Batur so that when you open the window of the room then you will see the beautiful view of Caldera with cool natural air which will certainly make you comfortable and will arouse the spirit to start tourism activities while in Kintamani.
